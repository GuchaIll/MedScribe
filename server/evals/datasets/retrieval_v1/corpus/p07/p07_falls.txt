GERIATRICS FALLS CLINIC NOTE
Date: 2026-03-25. Synthetic record for evaluation only.

80-year-old woman referred after two falls in three months, the second at night on the way to the bathroom, with a bruised hip but no fracture. She lives alone in a two-story house and her daughter visits on weekends.

Contributing factors identified
Zolpidem 10 mg nightly for 15 years.
Orthostatic hypotension: blood pressure 138/76 lying, 112/68 after three minutes standing, with lightheadedness.
Knee osteoarthritis with quadriceps weakness; she cannot rise from a chair without using her arms.
Bifocal glasses worn while walking on stairs.
Timed Up and Go test 16 seconds, above the 12 second fall-risk threshold.

Cognition: Montreal Cognitive Assessment 24 of 30, losing points on delayed recall and clock drawing.

Plan
Taper zolpidem by 2.5 mg every two weeks and teach sleep hygiene; cognitive behavioral therapy for insomnia referral. Reduce hydrochlorothiazide to address orthostasis. Home safety evaluation with grab bars and night lights. Physical therapy twice weekly for strength and balance. Single-vision distance glasses for walking outdoors. DEXA scan ordered.
